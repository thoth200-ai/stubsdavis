About Adam

Adam grew up in Stoneboro, Pennsylvania, a small western PA town where Penguins hockey isn't a hobby, it's a birthright. After graduating from Lakeview High School, he eventually made his way south, and these days he calls Wilmington, North Carolina home.

He's a business owner, too: Adam runs his own company, ILM Irrigation, keeping lawns and landscapes around Wilmington green. His customers adore him, including one sweet lady in her 90s whose doorbell is so loud that Adam says pressing it feels like being the president pushing the nuclear button.

In 2004, back when "You've got mail!" still meant something, Adam met his husband, Kevin, in an AOL chat room. What started as a conversation online turned into more than twenty years together. 2015 was a big year: they tied the knot in January, then found "the one" that summer after touring somewhere between 30 and 40 houses. A couple of years later their family grew, when their son, Johnathon, came home to them at age 10.

Born on Christmas Day, Adam has spent half a century sharing his birthday with the biggest holiday of the year, and he's never once let it steal his thunder. Adam doesn't just cheer for the Pens, either: he straps on the pads and plays goalie himself, and he's even taken home the occasional MVP award. Whether he's in the crease, cranking up an 80s anthem, or spending the weekend in a Hobbit hole, he does it all his own way.

Join us in celebrating 50 years of Adam!
